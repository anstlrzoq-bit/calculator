📐 Compounding Mathematics

1. Basic Lump-Sum Compound Interest

A = P × (1 + r/n)^(n×t)

Where P is initial principal, r is nominal annual rate, n is compounding periods per year, and t is time in years.

2. Recurring Contribution (Annuity Future Value)

FV_dca = PMT × [ ((1 + i)^N - 1) / i ]

Where PMT is periodic payment, i is effective periodic rate: (1 + r/n)^(n/f) - 1, and N is total payments.
